Reading:
- descent, grammarJson, deep, streamJson: growth per doubling about 2.0 on both implementations — LINEAR. No choice
  points, nothing retained behind the parse.
- termsOrElse, termsCommitted: allocation per term FLAT (1896 B, 2670-2752 B) — the work is linear — but time per term
  rises with n (machine 177 -> 320 ns orElse, 266 -> 734 ns committed, n 2000 -> 32000): both keep a live choice point
  per item until the parse ends, so the live heap grows with the input and the collector and caches pay for it.
  Not quadratic (that would be 4x per doubling; measured 2.1-2.6x).
- manyCommitted keeps its alternatives too: the commitment is checked when the other branch runs, the branch is not
  dropped. parsec drops it and runs in constant space. Recorded in BACKLOG as a perf item (a cut in Choose).
- the commit's A/B: grammarJson median 16622 us before, 16686 us after (+0.4%); streamJson 37158 -> 34459 us (-7%);
  both inside the noise at load ~10 — no measurable change from the expectations' attempts or the remembered stream.
- streamJson is ~2x grammarJson for the same text (4 KB chunks through the generator and the lifted stream): a
  constant, not growth; not looked into.
